AniCur

Guide

  1. Left-click on the canvas to paint (PAINT) or pick a color (PICK)
  2. Transp = erase to transparent; Solid = paint the current color
  3. ◀ ▶ switch frames, ＋ add, ⧉ duplicate current frame, ✕ delete
  4. The translucent pixels on the canvas are the next frame's onion skin
  5. The red cross is the hotspot (the click point). Click the HOTSPOT ✛ button to enter hotspot mode, then click/drag on the canvas to move it; switch back to PAINT so you can't nudge it by accident
  6. SELECT ⬚: drag to marquee a region, Copy (Ctrl+C), then Paste (Ctrl+V) and click where the copy's top-left should go — a ghost preview follows the pointer. Works across frames (copy, switch frame, paste); transparent pixels don't overwrite; Esc cancels; one paste = one undo step
  7. The recent-colors row remembers the last 8 colors you painted with — click to reuse
  8. ↶/↷ or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y: undo, redo (one stroke = one step)
  9. Import loads a local .cur/.ani (32×32, 32-bit), replacing the current project — undoable
  10. Export .cur saves the current frame; Export .ani saves all frames as an animation at the Preview panel's ms/frame rate (both include the hotspot)
  11. Hold a panel's title to drag it left/right and reorder the panels
